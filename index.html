Dark-mode surfaces

Dark and stone carry every role: they swap base and text between modes, both fully legible either way. Charcoal lifts cards off the dark base. Teal and sand are fills only, never text, since a midtone can't hold contrast against both a dark and a light background: put dark text on accent blocks. Nothing outside these five is used. Muted text, hairlines, and light-mode card surfaces are the dark or stone color at reduced opacity, not new tints.

Do

  • Pair dark and stone as base and text; flip them between modes.
  • Use teal and sand as fills: blocks, badges, graphic shapes.
  • Always put dark text on a teal or sand fill.
  • Let teal lead as the primary accent, sand support.

Don't

  • Set teal or sand as text: a midtone fails on one background.
  • Use teal and sand at equal weight; they compete.
  • Drop to pure white or pure black; keep the warm neutrals.
  • Add color beyond the five. The set is deliberately tight.
